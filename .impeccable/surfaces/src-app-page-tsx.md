# Home page

Scope: `/` (src/app/page.tsx). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a hobby LED maker deciding whether to install Design Studio. Action: download v0.7.0 for their OS. Proof: their own lights moving (episode footage, live pattern previews, the app's graph). Must survive: the pattern strip (live previews linking to /patterns and /upload). Must not feel like generic dark SaaS or toy-like. Hardware branch stays a clearly labelled preview. Episodes are not public yet: no YouTube links.

Colour scheme (user, after the first build): "Dark, new accents", "Tungsten warm", "No, one main accent", applied to the whole site. Tungsten amber replaces the series' per-episode hues; the series' ground, type and footage stay.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the home of the "Learn the Studio" series: hardware sitting in darkness, lit only by the pattern it runs. It refuses the split hero of copy beside an app screenshot with feature cards below.

OWN-WORLD: Studio-black ground (#0b0e12), no gradients, glass or panels; the only light is LED light from footage and live previews. One accent, Soft White 3000K (#ffb46b) from the Kelvin brand scale, for the action, step numerals and their slate rules, the hero headline's second line (the promise, lit like the slates' numerals), the tinted facts line, text links (amber is the link colour so links need no other device), the dotted links in the step 03 flow (the dotted signal cable from E11), the slate rule that closes the release notes in the download section, while the LED-dot wordmark and favicon sweep the full Kelvin scale, candle to sky (user request); candle (#ff8912) only for the preview status tag. Nothing else is amber. Warm neutrals for text. Audiowide for numerals and the closing line, Inter heavy for headlines, JetBrains Mono for data and captions. Stills sit unframed on the dark, feathered at the edges.

STORY: The visitor sees a matrix light up on a bench, reads the line "Build LED patterns with nodes. Send them to real lights.", downloads, or follows the four steps from graph to light, browses real patterns, and notes the hardware preview.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed silent loop from the E07 cold open (the first lit matrix in the series; the E01 cold open stays dark until its ident), matrix and board in the dark, weighted right. Left third: the headline in Inter 800 at display size, one sentence beneath, the OS download as the primary button in amber, "Browse patterns" beside it, a mono facts line in tinted amber below. A small footage credit sits bottom right as provenance. Nothing else above the fold.

FORM: Pinned by the user's own series after three rolled rounds. seed:c3723df81968b1e6 — user pin: "Yes, match the videos (Recommended)", after pointing at the episodes, YouTube art and app repo as inspiration.

Signature interaction: each numbered step's numeral and rule light up in amber as they enter view, like an episode slate. Motion: one fade-up of light per section (titles, stills, flow, closing line); reduced motion shows the lit state.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
